## What changed from your original

- **Plank:** step 5 is Plank, and the name is now **Phalakasana**. "Dandasana" was the wrong name, since that is the seated staff pose.
- **Rounds:** "Round A/B" is now "Side 1/Side 2", because one full round is both sides.
- **Leading leg:** I labelled it by which leg steps back first. Side 1 steps the right leg back, and Side 2 steps the left leg back.
- **Stretch vs. work:** stretched muscles now get a teal highlight and working muscles stay red. In the lunge, the back-leg hip flexors are now marked as stretched, not working, which fixes an error in the original.
- **Cobra glutes:** the glutes are marked as relaxed and secondary in Bhujangasana.
- **Lunge:** all lunge images use the classical back knee on the floor. Your original step 4 showed the knee lifted.
- **Breath:** Plank is "hold". For Ashtanga Namaskara I kept "exhale", where the breath is held out. Some schools use a normal breath in Plank or put Downward Dog at step 5, so adjust if your tradition differs.
- **Viewpoint:** every prompt specifies the practitioner's right side, facing right, so the near and far legs come out consistently.
